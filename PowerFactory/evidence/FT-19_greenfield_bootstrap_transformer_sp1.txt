Conclusion and limits:
This run validates the direct integration from an activated greenfield
bootstrap grid and buses to a newly created EvEMTBench-backed TypTr2 and
connected ElmTr2. It covers the preview and explicit-confirmation boundary,
50 Hz propagation, retained positive-sequence and nameplate values, type
assignment, both terminal connections, and safe out-of-service creation on
PowerFactory 2026 SP1.
