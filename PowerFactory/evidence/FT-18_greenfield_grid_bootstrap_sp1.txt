Conclusion and limits:
This run validates reuse and activation of a project-tree ElmNet, immediate
calculation-relevant discovery, retained grid frequency and bus attributes,
and idempotent repeat behavior on PowerFactory 2026 SP1. The grid and buses had
been created during the preceding bootstrap run; this record validates the
activation correction rather than repeating first-time creation. No load flow,
short-circuit calculation, dynamic simulation, diagram creation, equipment
connection, in-service energization, PowerFactory 2026 SP4 validation, or
independent revision verification was performed.
